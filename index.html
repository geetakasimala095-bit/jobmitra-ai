📝 How to Apply

  1. Open Odisha State Scholarship Portal.
  2. New student ହେଲେ Registration କରନ୍ତୁ।
  3. Aadhaar authentication complete କରନ୍ତୁ।
  4. Login କରନ୍ତୁ।
  5. Department & Scholarship Scheme select କରନ୍ତୁ।
  6. Student, Academic & Bank details fill କରନ୍ତୁ।
  7. Required documents upload କରନ୍ତୁ।
  8. Application preview check କରନ୍ତୁ।
  9. Final Submit କରି application copy save କରନ୍ତୁ।
